Our story

Why we started Fate of the Planet.

The thing you reach for every morning shouldn't be one of the things the planet can never get rid of.

Fate of the Planet began with that simple thought. ORO Smile is our answer: a toothbrush that does its job well, looks good on your sink, and returns to the soil when you're done with it.

We design and pack every brush in India, using bamboo and paper wherever we can. It's a small change, and we believe small changes, made by many people, decide the fate of the planet.

After use

From your sink, back to the soil.

  1. i.

    Brush for three months

    Replace your toothbrush about every three months, as most dentists recommend.

  2. ii.

    Remove the bristles

    Pull the bristles out with pliers and dispose of them separately.

  3. iii.

    Compost the handle

    Add the bamboo handle to your compost, or reuse it as a plant marker in the garden.
